>Here, I, Trinh Gia Huy, would like to share my most sincere feelings about the internship experience at AWS Vietnam, hoping this feedback can help the FCJ team and future generations of interns have better experiences.

### General Assessment

**1. Working Environment** The office workspace was quite professional and modern, with everything I needed to focus on managing the team's work and to communicate directly with members when needed.

**2. Mentor / Admin Team Support** The mentor provided very detailed guidance, explained clearly when I didn't understand, and always encouraged me to ask questions. The admin team supported procedures and documentation, and created favorable conditions for my work. I highly appreciated that the mentor let me try and solve problems myself instead of just giving the answers.

**3. Suitability of Work to Academic Major** My role managing the **Website Balan Coffee Workshop** project helped me build organizational and team coordination skills, and I also closely followed how the team implemented security measures in the project — something fairly closely related to the cybersecurity major I'm studying.

**4. Learning Opportunities & Skill Development** Since I study cybersecurity, I paid particular attention to how the team implemented authentication, access control, and secret management in the project, which gave me an additional practical perspective on application security beyond what I learned at school. In addition, the project management role helped me further develop planning skills, communication across members with different areas of expertise, and synthesizing technical information to make decisions.

**5. Culture & Team Spirit** The working culture here is quite open — team members were all enthusiastic, proactive about updating progress, and supportive of each other whenever issues came up, which made my coordination work much easier.

**6. Intern Policies / Benefits** The company provides support for an internship allowance and offers flexibility in working hours when necessary. FCJ team members are always ready to answer students' questions, even outside office hours. In addition, being able to participate in training workshops is a major plus.

### Other Questions

- What did you **enjoy most** during your internship?

    - What I enjoyed most was directly coordinating a project with many different technical areas, while also observing how the team applied security practices to a real project — very useful for my cybersecurity major.

- What do you think the company **needs to improve** for subsequent interns?

    - I think the company could organize more project management sharing sessions specifically for interns in this kind of role, to give them more tools and experience for handling real situations.

- If recommending to friends, would you **advise them to intern here**? Why?

    - Yes, definitely. This is a great environment to build project management skills while also broadening real-world knowledge of system security — well worth the experience.
